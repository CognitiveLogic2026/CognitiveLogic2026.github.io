QEN Framework · QEN Sovereign

Governed knowledge for verifiable decisions.

The QEN Framework structures governance, evidence, measurement and traceability. QEN Sovereign operates within a governed knowledge perimeter, preserving provenance, uncertainty, declared limits and human decision authority.

Evidence

Sources and provenance remain reviewable.

Control

Roles, oversight and decision records support accountability.

Human authority

QEN Sovereign supports analysis; it does not replace the accountable human decision-maker.
