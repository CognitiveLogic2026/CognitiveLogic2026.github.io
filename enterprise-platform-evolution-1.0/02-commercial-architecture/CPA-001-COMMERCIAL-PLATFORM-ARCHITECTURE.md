# CPA-001 — Commercial Platform Architecture

Status: PROPOSED
Implementation Authorized: NO

## Purpose

Definire l'architettura della Commercial Platform di Cognitive Logic come estensione governata della Enterprise Platform.

## Mission

Trasformare il patrimonio metodologico, editoriale e tecnico in servizi professionali, mantenendo indipendenza editoriale e rigore metodologico.

## Commercial Principles

- Evidence before marketing.
- Methodology before promotion.
- Editorial independence.
- No unsupported claims.
- Full traceability of services.

- AI Governance

## Commercial Assets

Ogni asset commerciale dovrà essere classificato come:

- Service
- Solution
- Vertical
- Training
- Assessment
- Workshop
- Research-based Offering

Evidence
→ Analysis
→ Architecture Review
→ Approval
→ Controlled Implementation
→ Verification
→ Documentation
