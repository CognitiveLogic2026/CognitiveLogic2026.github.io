# KPA-001 — Knowledge Platform Architecture

Status: PROPOSED
Implementation Authorized: NO

## Purpose

Definire l'architettura della Knowledge Platform come nucleo metodologico della Cognitive Logic Enterprise Platform.

## Mission

Organizzare, governare e rendere riutilizzabile la conoscenza prodotta dall'ecosistema Cognitive Logic.

## Knowledge Principles

- Knowledge must be intelligible.
- Sources must be traceable.
- Decisions must be explainable.
- Evidence must be verifiable.
- Relationships must be explicit.
- Terminology must be governed.
