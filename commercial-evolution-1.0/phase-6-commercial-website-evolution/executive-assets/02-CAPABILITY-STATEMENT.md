## Organisational Profile

Cognitive Logic is an independent AI Governance and Knowledge Governance initiative focused on the design of measurable, explainable and traceable decision systems.

Its purpose is to help organisations govern artificial intelligence as an enterprise capability rather than as an isolated technology deployment.

Cognitive Logic integrates:

- enterprise architecture;
- AI governance;
- knowledge governance;
- evidence management;
- decision traceability;
- regulatory readiness;
- organisational measurement;
- executive reporting.

The operating model is technology independent and provider independent.

## Core Capabilities

Cognitive Logic provides six integrated capability domains.

### AI Governance

Design and assessment of governance structures for artificial intelligence adoption, use and oversight.

This capability addresses:

- accountability;
- roles and responsibilities;
- decision authority;
- governance controls;
- risk management;
- regulatory alignment;
- continuous monitoring.

### Knowledge Governance

Identification, structuring and governance of the knowledge used by organisational and AI-assisted decision processes.

This capability addresses:

- knowledge sources;
- semantic consistency;
- information authority;
- organisational context;
- evidence lineage;
- knowledge quality;
- decision intelligibility.

### Decision Intelligence

Transformation of evidence, data and organisational knowledge into structured decision support.

This capability addresses:

- decision criteria;
- evidence relationships;
- explainable recommendations;
- governance indicators;
- executive interpretation;
- decision records;
- measurable outcomes.

### Explainability and Traceability

Design of mechanisms that make AI-assisted and governance decisions reviewable.

This capability addresses:

- source attribution;
- evidence traceability;
- decision rationale;
- responsibility mapping;
- governance controls;
- auditability;
- review mechanisms.

### Enterprise Assessment

Evidence-based evaluation of organisational governance capabilities, maturity and readiness.

Assessment capability includes:

- discovery;
- evidence collection;
- evidence validation;
- maturity measurement;
- gap identification;
- executive reporting;
- prioritised governance planning.

### Sovereign Intelligence

Application of QEN Sovereign Intelligence to maintain organisational control over evidence, knowledge, governance logic and decision structures.

This capability reduces dependency on individual AI models, technology platforms and external providers.

## Evidence and Validation

Cognitive Logic applies an evidence-first methodology.

Evidence used within assessments and decision processes must be:

- identifiable;
- relevant;
- attributable;
- reviewable;
- sufficiently current;
- connected to a documented purpose;
- evaluated according to defined validation criteria.

Recommendations are not based solely on opinion or unsupported interpretation.

The relationship between evidence, assessment findings, governance conclusions and executive decisions must remain traceable.

## Technology Independence

Cognitive Logic does not define governance around a single model, platform, provider or software product.

The architecture separates:

- organisational governance;
- knowledge structures;
- evidence sources;
- decision logic;
- technology implementation.

This allows governance capability to remain stable when technologies, providers or regulatory conditions change.

- Evidence First
- Governance First
- Measurement First
- Explainability
- Traceability
- Accountability
- Auditability
- Technology Independence
- Provider Independence
- Continuous Improvement

These principles apply across assessment, strategy, implementation and reporting activities.

## Engagement Models

Cognitive Logic supports progressive engagement models.

### Executive Discovery

A structured initial engagement designed to understand organisational context, priorities and governance challenges.

### Governance Assessment

An evidence-based evaluation of current capability, readiness, maturity or regulatory alignment.

### Executive Workshop

A facilitated engagement designed to establish shared governance vision, decision criteria and organisational alignment.

### Governance Strategy

A structured programme translating validated findings into priorities, initiatives, responsibilities and measurable outcomes.

### Governance Enablement

Implementation of explainability, traceability, evidence and decision-governance mechanisms.

### Continuous Governance

Ongoing review, measurement and improvement of organisational governance capability.
