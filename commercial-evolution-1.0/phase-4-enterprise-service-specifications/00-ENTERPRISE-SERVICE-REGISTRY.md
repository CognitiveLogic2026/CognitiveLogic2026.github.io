# Purpose

The Enterprise Service Registry is the authoritative index of the Cognitive Logic Enterprise Service Catalogue.

Its purpose is to:

- identify every Enterprise Service;
- define the official service sequence;
- establish service dependencies;
- provide a single governance reference;
- maintain consistency across the Commercial Evolution programme.

This registry is the official Source of Truth for all Enterprise Service Specifications.
