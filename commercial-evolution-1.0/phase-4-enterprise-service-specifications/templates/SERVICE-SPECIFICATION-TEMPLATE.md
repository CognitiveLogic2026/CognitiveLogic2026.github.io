# Editorial Guidelines

Every Enterprise Service Specification shall:

- follow this template without removing mandatory sections;
- use clear, measurable and professional language;
- define measurable customer value;
- identify evidence-based recommendations;
- maintain consistency with the Enterprise Service Registry;
- preserve terminology across the catalogue.

# Quality Checklist

Before publication verify that:

- all mandatory sections are present;

- dependencies are correct;
- KPIs are measurable;
- deliverables are complete;
- terminology is consistent;
- architectural references are aligned with ADR-CLE-004;
- the document is technology independent;
- editorial formatting is consistent;
- `git diff --check` reports no formatting errors.
