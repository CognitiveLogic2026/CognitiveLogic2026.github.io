# Customer Journey Mapping

Status: APPROVED

## Stage 1 — Awareness

Customer needs:

- comprendere il problema
- chiarire il valore della governance
- identificare rischi e opportunità

Services:

- Executive Discovery
- Governance Vision Workshop
- Executive Briefing

## Stage 2 — Diagnosis

Customer needs:

- conoscere la situazione corrente
- misurare maturità e gap
- individuare priorità

Services:

- AI Governance Assessment
- AI Act Readiness Assessment
- Knowledge Governance Assessment
- Data Intelligibility Assessment
- Decision Process Assessment

## Stage 3 — Design

Customer needs:

- definire il modello futuro
- assegnare responsabilità
- progettare conoscenza e decisioni

Services:

- AI Governance Strategy
- Knowledge Architecture
- Knowledge Graph Design
- Decision Intelligence Architecture
- Governance Operating Model

## Stage 4 — Activation

Customer needs:

- trasformare la strategia in strumenti operativi
- definire policy e KPI
- attivare la roadmap

Services:

- Governance Framework Design
- Governance Policy Design
- Governance KPI Framework
- Decision Traceability Design

## Stage 5 — Control

Customer needs:

- verificare efficacia e conformità
- produrre evidenze
- supportare il management

Services:

- Governance Review
- Governance Audit
- Executive Governance Dashboard Review

## Stage 6 — Continuous evolution

Customer needs:

- aggiornarsi
- migliorare
- prevenire nuovi rischi
- governare l'evoluzione

Services:

- Continuous Governance
- Regulatory Intelligence
- International Watch Executive Briefing
- Executive Strategic Advisory
