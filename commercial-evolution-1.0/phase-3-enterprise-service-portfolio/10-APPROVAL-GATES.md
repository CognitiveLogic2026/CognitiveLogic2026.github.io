# Approval Gates

Status: APPROVED

## Gate 1 — Portfolio Architecture

Approval criteria:

- maturity levels are coherent
- service families are distinct
- dependencies are documented
- sovereign architecture is preserved

## Gate 2 — Service Definition

Approval criteria:

- each service has a complete specification
- deliverables are explicit
- inputs and prerequisites are defined
- KPI are measurable
- overlaps are resolved

## Gate 3 — Commercial Readiness

Approval criteria:

- service catalogue is complete
- proposal templates exist
- qualification process is defined
- customer journey is commercially usable

## Gate 4 — Vertical Readiness

Approval criteria:

- sector-specific evidence exists
- terminology is appropriate
- KPI are adapted
- regulatory context is documented

## Gate 5 — Publication

Approval criteria:

- editorial review completed
- enterprise UX approved
- claims supported by evidence
- repository documentation aligned

## Gate 6 — Go-to-Market

Approval criteria:

- delivery capability confirmed
- scoping rules approved
- commercial governance approved
- feedback and review process active
