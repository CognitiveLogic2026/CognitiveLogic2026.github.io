## Purpose

This document defines the authoritative standards, structures and quality requirements for customer-facing deliverables produced within the Cognitive Logic Enterprise Delivery Framework.

Its purpose is to ensure that every deliverable provided to a customer is:

- clear;
- consistent;
- evidence based;
- explainable;
- traceable;
- measurable;
- professionally structured;
- aligned with approved governance and architectural principles.

The templates defined in this document establish a common delivery language across all Cognitive Logic Enterprise Services while preserving the flexibility required for service-specific objectives, customer contexts and contractual requirements.

## Scope

This standard applies to all customer-facing deliverables produced during the execution of Cognitive Logic Enterprise Services, including:

- executive reports;
- assessment reports;
- governance reports;
- strategic and operational roadmaps;
- executive presentations;
- evidence packages;
- decision-support artefacts;
- implementation recommendations;
- delivery closure documentation.

The standard applies across the complete delivery lifecycle and shall be used by all roles responsible for designing, reviewing, approving or issuing customer deliverables.

It governs the minimum required structure, content quality, evidence traceability, visual consistency, document control and approval requirements.

It does not prescribe a specific software platform, document editor, presentation system or external technology provider.
## Normative Alignment

All customer deliverables shall be developed in accordance with the approved Cognitive Logic architectural baseline, including:

- ADR-CLE-004 — QEN Sovereign Intelligence Principle;
- Enterprise Delivery Methodology (DF-001);
- Standard Delivery Lifecycle (DF-002);
- Service Governance Model (DF-003);
- Service Quality Framework (DF-014);
- Delivery Quality Assurance (DF-015);
- Continuous Improvement Framework (DF-016);
- Internal Delivery Playbooks (DF-017).

Where applicable, deliverables shall also support organizational compliance with relevant regulatory and governance frameworks, including AI governance, data governance and applicable international standards.

## Deliverable Principles

Every customer deliverable shall comply with the following principles.

### Executive Readability

Documents shall communicate complex assessments in a form understandable by executive stakeholders while preserving technical accuracy.

### Evidence-Based Content

Every significant statement, conclusion or recommendation shall be supported by intelligible data and verifiable evidence.

### Explainability

Assessment results, governance evaluations and recommendations shall be explainable through documented methodologies rather than opaque reasoning.

### Decision Traceability

Customers shall be able to understand how findings, scores and recommendations were produced.

### Consistency

Terminology, structure, formatting and visual presentation shall remain consistent across all Enterprise Services.

### Measurability

Deliverables shall include measurable indicators, assessment criteria and clearly defined success metrics whenever applicable.

### Technology Independence

Recommendations shall remain independent from specific software vendors, AI providers or implementation technologies unless explicitly required by contractual scope.

### Professional Quality

Every deliverable shall satisfy enterprise-grade standards for completeness, clarity, editorial quality and governance consistency.

## Executive Report Template

The Executive Report is intended for executive stakeholders and decision makers.

A standard Executive Report should include the following sections:

- Executive Summary
- Business Context
- Objectives
- Key Findings
- Strategic Risks
- Strategic Opportunities
- Governance Implications
- Executive Recommendations
- Next Steps
- Appendix (if required)

The report should emphasize clarity, concise communication and decision support while avoiding unnecessary technical detail.

## Assessment Report Template

Assessment Reports document structured evaluations performed during Enterprise Service delivery.

The recommended structure includes:

- Assessment Scope
- Assessment Methodology
- Evidence Sources
- Assessment Criteria
- Current State Analysis
- Findings
- Risk Analysis
- Maturity Evaluation
- Recommendations
- Improvement Priorities
- Evidence References
- Assessment Limitations

All assessment conclusions shall be supported by documented evidence and measurable evaluation criteria.

## Governance Report Template

Governance Reports communicate governance status, governance maturity and organizational recommendations.

The recommended structure includes:

- Governance Overview
- Governance Objectives
- Governance Model
- Organizational Responsibilities
- Decision Authorities
- Governance Findings
- Compliance Considerations
- Governance Risks
- Governance Recommendations
- Target Governance State
- Improvement Roadmap
- Governance KPI
- Evidence Register

Governance Reports shall provide decision-support information that is explainable, traceable and aligned with the approved governance framework.

## Roadmap Template

Implementation roadmaps communicate the recommended sequence of organizational improvements.

The recommended structure includes:

- Current State
- Target State
- Strategic Objectives
- Workstreams
- Major Activities
- Milestones
- Dependencies
- Risks
- Success Criteria
- KPI
- Estimated Timeline
- Expected Business Outcomes

Roadmaps shall prioritize initiatives according to business value, governance impact, organizational readiness and implementation feasibility.

## Executive Presentation Template

Executive presentations summarize delivery outcomes for management, steering committees and executive sponsors.

A standard presentation should include:

- Title
- Executive Summary
- Business Context
- Objectives
- Key Findings
- Strategic Insights
- Risks
- Recommendations
- Roadmap Overview
- Expected Benefits
- Closing Remarks
- Questions and Discussion

Presentation material should remain concise, visually consistent and focused on executive decision support.

## Evidence Package

The Evidence Package accompanies customer deliverables whenever traceability or assessment transparency is required.

It should include, where applicable:

- Evidence Register
- Evidence Classification
- Source References
- Assessment Traceability Matrix
- Supporting Documentation
- Decision Traceability References
- KPI Supporting Data
- Assumptions
- Constraints
- Version History

Evidence shall be organized to allow independent verification of the conclusions contained in the related customer deliverables.
## Quality Requirements

All customer deliverables shall satisfy the following minimum quality requirements.

### Completeness

Each deliverable shall include all mandatory sections required by its template and contractual scope.

### Accuracy

Statements, assessments and recommendations shall be technically correct and supported by validated information.

### Consistency

Terminology, document structure, scoring methodologies and governance concepts shall remain consistent across all deliverables.

### Evidence Integrity

Every assessment result shall be supported by intelligible data and verifiable evidence.

### Editorial Quality

Documents shall be grammatically correct, professionally formatted and suitable for executive audiences.

### Review and Approval

Customer deliverables shall undergo internal quality assurance before formal delivery.

## Branding and Consistency

All customer-facing documents shall comply with the Cognitive Logic visual and editorial standards.

This includes consistent use of:

- document structure;
- terminology;
- headings;
- typography;
- tables;
- diagrams;
- document metadata;
- versioning;
- branding elements.

Deliverables shall present a coherent enterprise identity across all services.

## Continuous Improvement

Customer deliverable templates shall be reviewed regularly to incorporate:

- customer feedback;
- lessons learned;
- quality review outcomes;
- governance improvements;
- architectural evolution;
- regulatory developments;
- enterprise best practices.

Approved improvements shall be incorporated through controlled document versioning.

## Architectural Notes

This document is an operational component of the Cognitive Logic Enterprise Delivery Framework.

Its purpose is to standardize customer-facing deliverables while preserving consistency with the approved Sovereign Intelligence Architecture.

All recommendations, findings and governance conclusions shall originate exclusively from:

- QEN Sovereign Engine;
- Governance Engine;
- Knowledge Graph;
- EVIDE;
- proprietary algorithms;
- intelligible data;
- verifiable evidence.

Customer deliverables shall never present opaque or non-traceable decision processes.
