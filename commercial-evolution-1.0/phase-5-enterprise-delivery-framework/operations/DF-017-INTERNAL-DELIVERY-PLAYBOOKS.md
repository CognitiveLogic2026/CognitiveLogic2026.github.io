# DF-017 — Internal Delivery Playbooks

| Field | Value |

## Purpose

This document defines the internal operational playbooks used to execute Cognitive Logic Enterprise Services consistently, efficiently and in accordance with approved governance, quality and architectural requirements.
## Scope

DF-017 applies to all internal delivery teams and defines the standard operational playbooks for:

- customer onboarding;
- delivery preparation;
- assessment execution;
- workshop facilitation;
- evidence collection;
- quality reviews;
- governance reviews;
- executive reporting;
- project closure;
- knowledge capture.

## Normative Alignment

DF-017 shall remain aligned with:

- DF-001 — Enterprise Delivery Methodology;
- DF-002 — Standard Delivery Lifecycle;
- DF-003 — Service Governance Model;
- DF-014 — Service Quality Framework;
- DF-015 — Delivery Quality Assurance;
- DF-016 — Continuous Improvement Framework;
- ADR-CLE-004 — QEN Sovereign Intelligence Principle.

## Playbook Principles

Every playbook shall be:

- repeatable;
- measurable;
- evidence-based;
- explainable;
- traceable;
- technology independent;
- continuously improvable.
## Standard Delivery Playbooks

### Customer Onboarding

Objective:

Prepare the engagement before execution begins.

Activities:

- confirm scope;
- identify stakeholders;
- validate prerequisites;
- schedule kickoff;
- assign delivery roles;
- create engagement workspace.

Outputs:

- confirmed scope;
- stakeholder register;
- delivery plan;
- kickoff completed.

### Delivery Preparation

Objective:

Ensure delivery readiness.

Activities:

- review applicable service specification;
- prepare templates;
- verify evidence sources;
- define quality controls;
- prepare governance checkpoints.

Outputs:

- delivery package;
- quality checklist;
- evidence plan;
- execution readiness confirmation.
### Assessment Execution

Objective:

Execute the assessment according to the approved methodology.

Activities:

- collect evidence;
- perform analysis;
- validate findings;
- document assumptions;
- record risks.

Outputs:

- assessment results;
- evidence register;
- preliminary findings.

### Executive Review

Objective:

Prepare executive decision support.

Activities:

- consolidate findings;
- validate recommendations;
- review quality;
- confirm traceability;
- prepare executive presentation.

Outputs:

- executive report;
- governance recommendations;
- decision package.

### Delivery Closure

Objective:

Complete the engagement in a controlled manner.

Activities:

- confirm deliverable acceptance;
- archive evidence;
- document lessons learned;
- register improvement opportunities;
- close the engagement.

Outputs:

- closure record;
- lessons learned;
- improvement backlog.

## Conformance

Every Enterprise Service shall execute the applicable playbooks defined by DF-017.

Documented deviations require formal approval according to the Service Governance Model.

## Continuous Improvement

Playbooks shall be reviewed periodically based on:

- delivery metrics;
- customer feedback;
- audit findings;
- quality assessments;
- governance reviews;
- organizational learning.

## Architectural Notes

Internal Delivery Playbooks operationalize the Enterprise Delivery Framework by translating governance, methodology and quality requirements into repeatable operational procedures.

The playbooks support consistent execution across all Enterprise Services while preserving evidence integrity, explainability, decision traceability and continuous organizational improvement.

This document is an authoritative operational component of the Commercial Evolution 1.0 Enterprise Delivery Framework and shall evolve only through approved governance processes.
