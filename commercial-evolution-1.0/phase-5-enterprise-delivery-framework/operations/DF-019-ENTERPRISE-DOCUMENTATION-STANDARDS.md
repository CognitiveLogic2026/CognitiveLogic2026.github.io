# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish the official enterprise documentation standards governing the creation, organization, maintenance, review, approval, evolution and retirement of documentation produced within the Commercial Evolution 1.0 programme.

These standards provide a single normative reference for documentation governance across all enterprise domains, ensuring that every document is developed according to consistent architectural, editorial and governance principles.

This document defines mandatory documentation standards rather than operational procedures.

Operational execution remains delegated to the specialized frameworks approved within the Enterprise Delivery Framework.

The standards defined herein aim to ensure that enterprise documentation is:

- consistent;
- traceable;
- explainable;
- measurable;
- auditable;
- maintainable;
- reusable;
- technology independent;
- provider independent;
- governance driven.

The Enterprise Documentation Standards constitute the normative foundation supporting long-term knowledge sustainability across the Cognitive Logic ecosystem.

---

# 2. Scope

These standards apply to every normative, governance, methodological, architectural, operational and supporting document developed within Commercial Evolution 1.0.

The scope includes, but is not limited to:

- Enterprise Delivery Framework documentation;
- Enterprise Service Catalogue documentation;
- Governance documentation;
- Architecture documentation;
- Standards;
- Methodologies;
- Frameworks;
- Policies;
- Guidelines;
- Reference models;
- Decision records;
- Knowledge repositories;
- Supporting documentation.

These standards govern the documentation itself rather than the operational processes described by individual documents.

Any future programme extending Commercial Evolution may adopt these standards without modification unless formally superseded through the governance process.

# 5. Documentation Governance Principles

Enterprise documentation shall comply with the following governing principles.

## 5.1 Single Source of Truth

Each governance concept shall be defined in exactly one authoritative document.

References shall be preferred over duplication.

---

## 5.2 Governance First

Documentation shall describe governance before operational execution.

Operational content shall inherit governance rather than redefine it.

---

## 5.3 Evidence First

Normative statements shall be supported by documented rationale, governance references or approved enterprise principles.

---

## 5.4 Explainability

Documentation shall enable readers to understand:

- purpose;
- responsibilities;
- decisions;
- dependencies;
- governance implications.

Implicit assumptions shall be avoided.

---

## 5.5 Traceability

Every normative statement shall be traceable to:

- governance;
- architecture;
- methodology;
- approved enterprise decisions.

---

## 5.6 Consistency

Equivalent concepts shall be documented consistently throughout the repository.

Terminology shall never vary without formal justification.

---

## 5.7 Technology Independence

Documentation shall avoid implementation-specific dependencies unless explicitly required.

---

## 5.8 Provider Independence

Documentation shall remain independent from software vendors, cloud providers and commercial technologies.

---

## 5.9 Framework Independence

Documentation standards shall remain reusable beyond Commercial Evolution whenever possible.

---

## 5.10 Continuous Evolution

Documentation shall evolve through controlled governance rather than uncontrolled incremental modifications.

- Programme
- Phase
- Domain
- Version
- Status
- Document Owner
- Approved By
- Classification
- Effective Date
- Review Frequency
- Next Review
- Related Documents
- Repository

Additional metadata may be introduced where justified by governance requirements, provided they remain consistent with enterprise documentation standards.

---

## 10.2 Metadata Consistency

Metadata values shall remain consistent with:

- Repository Registry
- Document lifecycle status
- Governance approvals
- Version history
- Document ownership

---

## 10.3 Metadata Governance

Metadata modifications shall follow the same governance process applicable to document content.

Unauthorized metadata changes are prohibited.

# 47. Conformance Assessment

Conformance assessments evaluate the degree to which documentation complies with enterprise standards.

Assessments support governance improvement rather than individual performance evaluation.

## 47.1 Assessment Scope

Conformance assessments may evaluate:

- documentation completeness;
- governance compliance;
- repository consistency;
- editorial quality;
- traceability;
- auditability;
- documentation maturity.

---

## 47.2 Assessment Evidence

Assessment conclusions shall be supported by objective and reviewable evidence.

---

## 47.3 Corrective Actions

Identified non-conformities shall be managed through documented corrective actions consistent with enterprise change management processes.

---

# 48. Annex

This document establishes the normative foundation governing enterprise documentation throughout Commercial Evolution 1.0.

Specialized frameworks may extend documentation practices within their respective domains provided that such extensions:

- remain consistent with these standards;
- preserve enterprise governance principles;
- avoid duplication of normative requirements;
- maintain traceability and auditability;
- support evidence-based governance;
- remain technology independent;
- remain provider independent;
- remain explainable and measurable.

The Enterprise Documentation Standards shall remain the authoritative reference governing documentation architecture, lifecycle, quality and governance across the Commercial Evolution programme until formally superseded through the approved enterprise governance process.
