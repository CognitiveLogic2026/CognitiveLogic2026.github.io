## Purpose

This document defines the enterprise quality model applicable to every Cognitive Logic delivery engagement.

Quality shall be measurable, explainable, traceable, evidence-based, auditable and suitable for executive use.

## Scope

The framework applies to:

- delivery processes;
- quality assurance;
- quality control;
- quality gates;
- deliverables;
- evidence;
- decisions;
- documentation;
- architecture;
- compliance;
- risk;
- KPI;
- audits;
- corrective actions;
- continuous improvement.

## Normative Alignment

DF-014 shall remain consistent with:

- DF-001 — Enterprise Delivery Methodology;
- DF-002 — Standard Delivery Lifecycle;
- DF-003 — Service Governance Model;
- ADR-CLE-004 — QEN Sovereign Intelligence Principle.

## Sovereign Intelligence Alignment

Authoritative quality decisions derive exclusively from:

- QEN Sovereign Engine;
- Governance Engine;
- Knowledge Graph;
- EVIDE;
- proprietary algorithms;
- intelligible data;
- verifiable evidence;
- authorized human review.

No external AI provider constitutes an authoritative decision source.

## Quality Principles

- Evidence First
- Quality by Design
- Independent Review
- Segregation of Duties
- Measurability
- Proportionality
- Traceability
- Explainability
- Prevention before Correction
- Continuous Improvement

## Quality Objectives

| Objective | Required Outcome |
|---|---|
| Completeness | All required content and records are present. |
| Correctness | Outputs accurately reflect evidence and analysis. |
| Consistency | Common methods and standards are applied. |
| Traceability | Outcomes link to requirements, evidence and decisions. |
| Explainability | Findings and approvals are understandable. |
| Verifiability | Independent reviewers can validate conclusions. |
| Auditability | Records support retrospective review. |
| Compliance | Applicable obligations are satisfied. |
| Usability | Deliverables are fit for purpose. |
| Improvement | Quality performance strengthens over time. |

## Quality Governance

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Enterprise Governance Board | Approves policy and major exceptions. |
| Quality Review Board | Approves material quality gates. |
| Quality Manager | Maintains controls, metrics and reporting. |
| Quality Lead | Coordinates engagement quality. |
| Delivery Lead | Ensures execution conformance. |
| Independent Reviewer | Performs objective review. |
| Internal Auditor | Evaluates control effectiveness. |

No individual shall create, review and approve the same material outcome.

## Quality Assurance

Quality Assurance shall include:

- quality planning;
- method validation;
- competence verification;
- lifecycle readiness checks;
- evidence planning;
- peer review;
- architecture alignment;
- compliance control design;
- preventive actions.

## Quality Control

Quality Control shall include:

- completeness checks;
- evidence validation;
- consistency checks;
- calculation verification;
- traceability testing;
- architecture review;
- KPI reproduction;
- editorial review;
- acceptance testing;
- approval verification.

## Quality Gates

Each lifecycle phase shall end with a documented Quality Gate.

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| PASS | Progression authorized. |
| PASS WITH CONDITIONS | Progression authorized with controlled actions. |
| REWORK REQUIRED | Progression suspended pending remediation. |
| FAIL | Progression prohibited and escalation required. |

Each gate shall record:

- objective;
- inputs;
- controls;
- acceptance criteria;
- reviewer;
- approval authority;
- evidence;
- findings;
- decision;
- rationale;
- remediation.

## Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance criteria shall be:

- specific;
- measurable;
- evidence-based;
- testable;
- traceable;
- approved before final review;
- proportionate to risk.

Acceptance shall never be inferred from silence or informal communication.

## Deliverable Quality

Every deliverable shall be reviewed for:

- completeness;
- accuracy;
- consistency;
- evidence support;
- explainability;
- traceability;
- usability;
- version integrity;
- approval status.

## Evidence Quality

Evidence shall be evaluated for:

- provenance;
- authenticity;
- relevance;
- completeness;
- currency;
- accuracy;
- consistency;
- integrity;
- accessibility;
- verifiability.

Unsupported or unverifiable evidence shall not support a material decision.

## Decision Quality

Every material decision shall record:

- authority;
- scope;
- evidence;
- criteria;
- alternatives;
- assumptions;
- risks;
- rationale;
- consequences;
- approval;
- traceability.

## Documentation Quality

Documentation shall be:

- accurate;
- current;
- uniquely identified;
- version-controlled;
- consistently structured;
- readable;
- traceable;
- approved;
- retained.

No TODO, placeholder or empty section is permitted.

## Architecture Quality

Architecture reviews shall verify:

- compliance with approved principles;
- sovereign decision authority;
- technology independence;
- explainability;
- traceability;
- evidence integrity;
- maintainability;
- ADR-CLE-004 alignment.

## Compliance Quality

Compliance review shall verify:

- applicable obligations;
- mapped controls;
- required evidence;
- assigned responsibilities;
- documented deviations;
- remediation status;
- residual exposure;
- authorized approval.

## Risk-Based Quality

| Level | Risk | Minimum Control |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Low | Self-check and completion review |
| Q2 | Moderate | Peer review and Quality Lead validation |
| Q3 | High | Independent review and Quality Review Board approval |
| Q4 | Critical | Independent assurance and Enterprise Governance approval |

## KPI Quality

Quality KPI shall be measurable, reproducible, evidence-based, decision-relevant and assigned to an owner.

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Quality Gate Pass Rate | ≥ 98% |
| Deliverable Acceptance Rate | ≥ 95% |
| Evidence Coverage | 100% |
| Decision Traceability | 100% |
| Documentation Conformance | ≥ 98% |
| Corrective Action Closure | ≥ 95% |
| Audit Conformance | ≥ 95% |

## Non-Conformity Management

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Minor | Limited deviation without material impact |
| Major | Material failure affecting quality or governance |
| Critical | Unacceptable risk requiring immediate escalation |

Every non-conformity shall have an owner, classification, root cause, action, deadline and closure evidence.

## Corrective Actions

Corrective actions shall eliminate identified causes and prevent recurrence.

Closure requires implementation evidence and effectiveness verification.

## Preventive Actions

Preventive actions shall address emerging risks, recurring defects, audit observations and control weaknesses.

## Executive Review

Executive review shall assess:

- KPI;
- audit findings;
- non-conformities;
- corrective actions;
- quality risks;
- recurring defects;
- exceptions;
- improvement priorities.

## Audit Model

Quality audits shall follow:

1. Planning
2. Scope Definition
3. Evidence Collection
4. Control Testing
5. Findings
6. Reporting
7. Corrective Action
8. Verification
9. Closure

## Quality Reporting

Minimum reports:

- engagement quality status;
- quality gate record;
- KPI report;
- non-conformity report;
- corrective action report;
- portfolio quality report;
- management review record.

## Continuous Improvement

Improvement shall follow an evidence-driven cycle:

1. Measure
2. Analyse
3. Prioritize
4. Approve
5. Implement
6. Verify
7. Standardize
8. Record

## Quality Maturity Model

| Level | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Initial | Quality depends on individual effort. |
| 2 | Controlled | Basic controls and records exist. |
| 3 | Standardized | Common gates, methods and metrics are applied. |
| 4 | Measured | Performance is quantitatively monitored. |
| 5 | Optimized | Quality continuously improves through evidence and governance. |

## Conformance

An engagement is conformant where:

- a Quality Plan exists;
- quality gates are executed;
- acceptance criteria are applied;
- evidence supports decisions;
- independent reviews are complete;
- non-conformities are managed;
- approvals are authorized;
- quality records are retained;
- KPI are reported;
- improvement actions are initiated.

## Architectural Notes

DF-014 translates DF-001 principles into measurable controls, applies them across DF-002 and operates under DF-003 governance.

Quality is treated as a governed enterprise system, not as a final editorial check.

DF-014 provides the baseline for DF-015 and DF-016.
