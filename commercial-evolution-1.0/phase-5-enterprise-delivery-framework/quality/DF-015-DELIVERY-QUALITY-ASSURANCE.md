## Purpose

This document defines the assurance process used to verify that every delivery engagement conforms to approved quality, governance, evidence and architectural requirements.

## Scope

DF-015 applies to:

- delivery planning;
- lifecycle execution;
- evidence management;
- quality gates;
- deliverables;
- decisions;
- approvals;
- non-conformities;
- closure.

## Normative Alignment

DF-015 shall remain aligned with:

- DF-001 — Enterprise Delivery Methodology;
- DF-002 — Standard Delivery Lifecycle;
- DF-003 — Service Governance Model;
- DF-014 — Service Quality Framework;
- ADR-CLE-004 — QEN Sovereign Intelligence Principle.

## Assurance Principles

- independence;
- evidence-based verification;
- segregation of duties;
- proportionality;
- traceability;
- explainability;
- measurability;
- auditability;
- prevention;
- continuous improvement.

## Assurance Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Quality Manager | Owns assurance policy and controls. |
| Quality Lead | Plans engagement assurance. |
| Delivery Lead | Ensures execution conformity. |
| Independent Reviewer | Performs objective verification. |
| Evidence Owner | Ensures evidence integrity. |
| Service Owner | Accepts service quality accountability. |
| Governance Board | Approves major exceptions and escalations. |

No individual shall independently produce, review and approve the same material outcome.

## Assurance Planning

Every engagement shall define:

- assurance scope;
- risk level;
- applicable controls;
- review frequency;
- quality gates;
- evidence requirements;
- reviewers;
- approval authorities;
- escalation paths;
- reporting cadence.

## Assurance Levels

| Level | Risk | Minimum Assurance |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Low | Self-check and completion review |
| A2 | Moderate | Peer review and Quality Lead validation |
| A3 | High | Independent review and Quality Review Board approval |
| A4 | Critical | Independent assurance and executive approval |

## Lifecycle Assurance

Assurance shall verify each delivery phase before progression.

### Engagement Assurance

Verify:

- authorized scope;
- stakeholder identification;
- responsibilities;
- assumptions;
- constraints;
- contractual alignment.

### Discovery Assurance

Verify:

- completeness of inputs;
- source reliability;
- stakeholder coverage;
- evidence gaps;
- documented limitations.

### Assessment Assurance

Verify:

- approved methodology;
- consistent criteria;
- reproducible scoring;
- evidence linkage;
- reviewer competence.

### Analysis Assurance

Verify:

- analytical correctness;
- assumption transparency;
- risk consideration;
- explainability;
- traceability.

### Recommendation Assurance

Verify:

- evidence support;
- proportionality;
- feasibility;
- dependencies;
- expected outcomes;
- residual risks.

### Reporting Assurance

Verify:

- factual accuracy;
- executive relevance;
- consistency;
- decision traceability;
- version integrity;
- approval status.

### Closure Assurance

Verify:

- deliverable acceptance;
- open-action ownership;
- evidence retention;
- lessons learned;
- closure approval.

## Quality Gate Assurance

Each quality gate shall record:

- gate identifier;
- phase;
- mandatory controls;
- acceptance criteria;
- evidence;
- reviewer;
- findings;
- decision;
- rationale;
- remediation;
- approval.

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| PASS | Progression authorized |
| PASS WITH CONDITIONS | Progression authorized with controlled actions |
| REWORK REQUIRED | Progression suspended |
| FAIL | Progression prohibited and escalation required |

## Evidence Assurance

Evidence shall be verified for:

- provenance;
- authenticity;
- relevance;
- completeness;
- currency;
- accuracy;
- integrity;
- accessibility;
- verifiability.

Unsupported evidence shall not support material findings or decisions.

## Deliverable Assurance

Every deliverable shall be reviewed for:

- purpose alignment;
- completeness;
- correctness;
- evidence support;
- explainability;
- traceability;
- usability;
- documentation quality;
- approval authority.

## Decision Assurance

Material decisions shall include:

- authority;
- scope;
- evidence;
- criteria;
- alternatives;
- assumptions;
- risks;
- rationale;
- consequences;
- approval;
- traceability.

## Documentation Assurance

Documentation checks shall verify:

- document ID;
- version;
- status;
- ownership;
- heading structure;
- terminology;
- internal references;
- table integrity;
- approval record;
- absence of unresolved markers;
- repository conformity.

## Architectural Assurance

Architecture review shall verify:

- sovereign decision authority;
- technology independence;
- explainability;
- evidence integrity;
- traceability;
- maintainability;
- ADR-CLE-004 alignment.

No external AI provider shall constitute an authoritative decision source.

## Non-Conformity Assurance

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Minor | Limited deviation without material impact |
| Major | Material failure affecting quality or governance |
| Critical | Unacceptable risk requiring immediate escalation |

Each non-conformity shall include:

- owner;
- classification;
- impact;
- root cause;
- corrective action;
- deadline;
- closure evidence;
- approval.

## Assurance Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Mandatory Review Completion | 100% |
| Evidence Coverage | 100% |
| Decision Traceability | 100% |
| Quality Gate Compliance | ≥ 98% |
| Corrective Action Closure | ≥ 95% |
| Documentation Conformance | ≥ 98% |
| Audit Conformance | ≥ 95% |

## Assurance Reporting

Minimum reports include:

- engagement assurance status;
- quality gate record;
- non-conformity report;
- corrective action status;
- assurance KPI report;
- executive escalation report;
- closure assurance record.

## Escalation

Escalation is mandatory where:

- evidence integrity is compromised;
- a critical non-conformity exists;
- a gate fails repeatedly;
- reviewer independence is impaired;
- architecture or compliance requirements are violated;
- corrective action is overdue;
- residual risk exceeds tolerance.

## Audit Trail

The assurance trail shall preserve:

- reviewed artefacts;
- evidence examined;
- controls executed;
- findings;
- decisions;
- reviewers;
- approvals;
- dates;
- corrective actions;
- closure records.

## Conformance

An engagement conforms to DF-015 where:

- assurance planning exists;
- controls are proportionate to risk;
- required reviews are complete;
- quality gates are recorded;
- evidence supports outcomes;
- non-conformities are managed;
- approvals are authorized;
- audit records are retained.

## Architectural Notes

DF-015 operationalizes DF-014 by defining how quality is independently verified during delivery.

It applies assurance across DF-002 and operates under DF-003 governance.

DF-015 provides assurance evidence for DF-016 continuous improvement.
