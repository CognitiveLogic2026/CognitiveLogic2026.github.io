## Executive Summary

The Service Governance Model establishes the official governance baseline for the Cognitive Logic Enterprise Delivery Framework.

Its purpose is to define how governance authority is assigned, exercised, monitored and audited throughout every enterprise delivery activity.

This document defines governance roles, accountability, decision authority, approval responsibilities, segregation of duties, escalation mechanisms and governance controls independently from the service specifications and independently from the delivery lifecycle defined in DF-002.

Every governance decision shall be attributable to an authorized role, supported by intelligible data and verifiable evidence, and remain fully traceable throughout the delivery lifecycle.

The governance model is fully aligned with DF-001, DF-002 and ADR-CLE-004 and recognizes only the Sovereign Intelligence Architecture as authoritative decision support.

## Purpose

The purpose of DF-003 is to establish the authoritative governance model governing every enterprise delivery activity executed within the Cognitive Logic Enterprise Delivery Framework.

The model ensures consistent governance, explicit accountability, controlled decision-making, evidence-based approvals and measurable organizational responsibility across all enterprise services.

## Scope

DF-003 applies to all enterprise services, delivery engagements, governance activities, quality reviews, architectural decisions, operational controls and delivery governance processes defined within Commercial Evolution 1.0.

The document governs organizational authority rather than technical implementation or service-specific methodologies.

## Governance Principles

The governance model is founded upon the following principles:

- explicit authority;
- single-point accountability;
- evidence-based governance;
- decision traceability;
- segregation of duties;
- proportional governance;
- independent quality assurance;
- controlled change;
- measurable governance;
- continuous improvement;
- sovereign decision authority in accordance with ADR-CLE-004.

## Governance Structure

The governance structure is organized across strategic, tactical and operational levels to ensure clear authority, independent oversight and effective execution.

## Governance Layers

The Enterprise Delivery Framework is governed through Executive Governance, Delivery Governance, Quality Governance and Operational Governance, each with defined responsibilities and decision boundaries.

## Organizational Roles

Organizational roles define governance ownership, decision authority, accountability and oversight responsibilities across the Enterprise Delivery Framework.

## Delivery Roles

Delivery roles execute approved activities, produce governed artefacts, maintain delivery evidence and support governance decisions within their assigned authority.

## Governance Bodies

Governance Bodies provide strategic direction, approve major decisions, resolve escalations and preserve governance consistency across all enterprise services.

## Decision Authorities

Every material decision shall be assigned to an explicitly authorized governance role or governance body. Decision authority shall never be assumed implicitly and shall remain fully traceable.

## Responsibility Model

Responsibilities define the activities assigned to each governance and delivery role. Every responsibility shall have defined inputs, outputs, authority limits and measurable outcomes.

## Accountability Model

Each governed outcome shall have a single accountable owner responsible for ensuring that objectives, governance requirements and quality expectations are achieved.

## Segregation of Duties

No individual shall independently perform, review and approve the same material governance activity. Independent review shall be applied whenever governance, quality or risk could be compromised.

## Escalation Model

Escalation is mandatory when decision authority is exceeded, governance conflicts arise, evidence is insufficient or delivery risks exceed approved tolerance levels.

## Approval Model

Approvals shall be granted only by authorized governance roles after verification that applicable governance, quality, architecture and evidence requirements have been satisfied.

## Quality Governance

Quality Governance ensures that deliverables comply with approved standards before lifecycle progression is authorized.

## Evidence Governance

Evidence Governance guarantees that every governance decision is supported by intelligible, verifiable and auditable evidence preserved throughout the delivery lifecycle.

## Decision Governance

Decision Governance requires every material decision to record its authority, rationale, supporting evidence, approval status and resulting actions.

## Risk Governance

Risk Governance ensures that delivery risks are identified, evaluated, assigned, monitored and mitigated through documented governance controls and defined ownership.

## Change Governance

All changes affecting approved baselines shall undergo formal impact assessment, authorization and controlled implementation before becoming effective.

## Architecture Governance

Architecture Governance ensures continuous alignment with DF-001, DF-002, ADR-CLE-004 and the Sovereign Intelligence Architecture throughout every delivery engagement.

## Operational Governance

Operational Governance maintains governance effectiveness during execution through continuous monitoring, reporting, issue management and corrective actions.

## Lifecycle Governance

Each lifecycle stage shall be entered and exited only after the applicable governance gates, approvals and quality controls have been successfully completed.

## Cross-Service Governance

Cross-Service Governance coordinates dependencies, shared artefacts, governance consistency and organizational alignment across multiple enterprise services.

## KPI Governance

Governance effectiveness shall be measured through defined KPIs covering quality, evidence completeness, decision timeliness, compliance and delivery performance.

## Audit Governance

Audit Governance guarantees that governance activities remain independently verifiable through complete records, traceable decisions and preserved evidence.

## Compliance Governance

Compliance Governance ensures continuous conformity with internal policies, contractual commitments and applicable regulatory requirements.

## Conformance Model

The Conformance Model defines how governance adherence is assessed, deviations are managed, corrective actions are tracked and continuous governance improvement is achieved.

## Governance Principles — Detailed Principles

### Governance by Accountability

Every governance activity shall have one and only one accountable owner.

Accountability cannot be transferred implicitly and remains identifiable throughout the entire delivery lifecycle.

### Evidence First

Governance decisions shall be based on intelligible data, documented rationale and verifiable evidence.

No governance approval shall rely solely on undocumented assumptions or informal communications.

### Independent Oversight

Governance oversight shall remain organizationally independent from delivery execution whenever quality, risk or architecture approval is required.

### Transparency

Governance responsibilities, approvals, exceptions and escalations shall remain transparent to all authorized stakeholders.

### Consistency

Equivalent governance situations shall be managed through equivalent governance decisions unless an approved exception exists.

### Traceability

Every governance action shall be reconstructable through documented evidence, governance artefacts and decision records.

### Controlled Delegation

Delegated authority shall be documented, time-bounded, explicitly accepted and revocable.

Delegation transfers authority within defined limits but does not remove accountability from the delegating authority unless explicitly approved.

### Continuous Improvement

Governance effectiveness shall be reviewed through audits, KPIs, lessons learned and corrective actions in order to strengthen the Enterprise Delivery Framework over time.

## Governance Bodies — Detailed Definition

### Enterprise Governance Board

The Enterprise Governance Board is the highest governance authority of the Enterprise Delivery Framework.

Its responsibilities include:

- resolving governance escalations;
- approving governance exceptions;
- supervising governance maturity;
- reviewing strategic KPIs.

### Architecture Review Board

The Architecture Review Board ensures architectural consistency across all Enterprise Services.

Its responsibilities include:

- architecture reviews;
- baseline conformance;
- approval of architectural deviations;
- governance of reusable architectural assets.

### Delivery Governance Committee

The Delivery Governance Committee supervises delivery execution.

Its responsibilities include:

- delivery oversight;
- quality monitoring;
- governance reporting;
- issue escalation;
- corrective action tracking.

### Quality Review Board

The Quality Review Board independently evaluates delivery quality before lifecycle progression.

It operates independently from delivery execution to preserve objective governance.

### Authority Principles

Decision authority shall always be:

- explicitly assigned;
- documented;
- traceable;
- independently reviewable;
- exercised within approved governance boundaries.

No governance authority may be inferred from organizational seniority alone.

Authority exists only where explicitly defined by this governance model or by formally approved delegation.

## Escalation Framework

### Escalation Levels

| Level | Trigger | Decision Authority | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Operational issue | Delivery Lead | Resolution within delivery team |
| L2 | Cross-functional issue | Service Owner | Coordinated resolution |
| L3 | Governance conflict | Delivery Governance Committee | Governance decision |
| L4 | Strategic impact | Enterprise Governance Board | Executive decision |

### Escalation Principles

Escalation shall be initiated whenever:

- governance authority is exceeded;
- delivery objectives cannot be achieved within approved tolerances;
- significant architectural deviations are identified;
- quality gates cannot be satisfied;
- evidence is incomplete or unreliable;
- regulatory or contractual risks emerge.

Every escalation shall include:

- issue description;
- business impact;
- supporting evidence;
- proposed options;
- recommended decision;
- accountable owner;
- target resolution date.

## Governance Glossary

For the purposes of this document:

- Governance: the system of authority, accountability and oversight governing delivery.
