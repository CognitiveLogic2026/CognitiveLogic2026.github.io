The Formula

An equation that measures quality across three dimensions.

The QEN score results from three weighted components capturing the fundamental value dimensions: historical, artisanal and territorial.

QEN = (Vs × 0.40) + (Va × 0.35) + (Vt × 0.25)
Vs — Historical Value Depth of tradition, documented historical continuity, generational transmission of knowledge. dominant weight · 40%
Va — Artisanal Value Quality of the production process, technical mastery, authenticity of method and uniqueness of output. secondary weight · 35%
Vt — Territorial Value Rooting in geographic context, local identity, short supply chain and ecosystem belonging. complementary weight · 25%

Algorithms

The computational engines powering the QEN graph.

Each algorithm operates on a specific subset of the graph, producing partial scores that feed into the aggregated QEN.

ALGO · 01

ColtFragr

Colt Frag · Ethical Logistics. Evaluates the logistical quality and territorial ethics of the operating model relative to its reference territory.

Input: Vs · Vt
ALGO · 02

TradRad

Rooted Tradition. Measures the historical depth and continuity of artisanal practices against a territory's documented standards.

Input: Vs · Va
ALGO · 03

EcoLocal

Local Ecosystem. Quantifies integration within the territorial network: short supply chain, producer relationships, impact on the local community.

Input: Va · Vt
