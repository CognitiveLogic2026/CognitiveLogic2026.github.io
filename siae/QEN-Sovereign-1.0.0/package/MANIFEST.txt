DESCRIPTION
QEN Sovereign Intelligence is a sovereign, deterministic and
evidence-driven intelligence architecture derived from the evolution
of the QEN Framework.

The system provides governed functions for:
- QEN scoring
- AI risk classification
- entity assessment
- compliance audit
- territorial mapping
- advisory assessment
- governed Knowledge Graph evidence retrieval
- provider-independent authoritative decision support

ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE
Authoritative decisions derive from governed knowledge assets,
proprietary rules, validated evidence and intelligible data.
External AI providers are not authoritative decision authorities.

RELATION TO QEN FRAMEWORK
QEN Sovereign Intelligence represents an evolutionary software and
governance development of the previously existing QEN Framework.

The QEN Framework remains the methodological foundation.
QEN Sovereign introduces an autonomous sovereign intelligence engine,
provider-independent decision architecture, governed evidence
retrieval and dedicated runtime integration.
