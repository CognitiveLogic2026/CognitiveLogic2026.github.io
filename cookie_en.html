Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Version 1.1

This document describes the types of cookies and similar technologies used by cognitivelogic.it, in accordance with the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC (as amended by 2009/136/EC), the GDPR (EU Reg. 2016/679) and the Italian Data Protection Authority's Order of 10 June 2021 (no. 231).

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website sends to the user's browser, where they are stored and then sent back to the site on the next visit. Similar technologies such as pixels, web beacons and local storage may also be used.

2. Cookies used by this site

cognitivelogic.it is a static informational website. It does not use its own profiling cookies or analytics cookies. Full details below:

Name / Type Category Provider Purpose Duration
cl_cookie_consent Technical / necessary Cognitive Logic (1st party) Stores the user's choice on the cookie banner 365 days

No profiling cookies, analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics), social media cookies or remarketing pixels are present.

3. Typefaces

The fonts used across most of the site (Cormorant Garamond, DM Mono) are hosted directly on this domain (self-hosted): no request is made to any third-party server to load them, and no consent is required.

A limited number of internal/tool pages (alert-engine.html, qen-widget.html, qen-prompt-generator.html, mascot.html) still load specific typefaces from Google Fonts (Google LLC servers, USA); this HTTP request may communicate the user's IP address to Google, without setting any cookie. Consent for these pages is requested via the banner shown on first visit.

4. Technical cookies and consent

Technical cookies necessary for the site to function (such as cl_cookie_consent) do not require consent under Art. 122 of Legislative Decree 196/2003 and the Italian DPA Order of 10/06/2021.
