Purpose

IW-EVM establishes a controlled path from a bounded claim to an auditable conclusion. It keeps claims, evidence, interpretation and operational recommendations distinct so that each passage can be reviewed independently.

Applied verification case

A practical application of this methodology is available in the International Watch analysis “Il 53,7% delle aziende agricole è davvero circolare? Dal dato ISTAT al claim verificabile” . The case reconstructs how an authoritative statistical source can remain methodologically sound while a subsequent claim may exceed what the underlying evidence actually establishes.

Verification dimensions

  • source provenance and authenticity;
  • relevance and temporal validity;
  • triangulation and management of conflicting evidence;
  • confidence assessment and explicit limitations;
  • decision records and reconstructable audit trails;
  • preservation of human authority and accountability.

Deposited research package

The Zenodo record contains the publication PDF, an editable DOCX version, citation and licensing information, a machine-readable JSON Schema, a synthetic demonstration dataset, repository metadata and SHA-256 integrity checksums.

Relationship to Cognitive Logic research

IW-EVM is the operational evidence-verification methodology used by International Watch. It complements Cognitive Logic research on Evidence Governance, decision traceability, QEN Framework and QEN Sovereign, but it does not replace or redefine those separate works.
