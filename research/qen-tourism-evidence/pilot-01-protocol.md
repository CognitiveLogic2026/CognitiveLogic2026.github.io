# Pilot 01 Protocol
## Coastal Tourism Evidence Assessment

**Pilot ID:** QEN-TEA-P01
**Status:** DESIGN DRAFT
**Date:** 9 October 2026
**Publication status:** Internal research; not approved for publication.

## Purpose

Pilot 01 tests whether the Tourism Evidence Assessment can transform a
limited set of public claims into a traceable evidence record without
turning visibility, documentation or marketing statements into proof.

The pilot belongs to the existing QEN Sovereign Tourism Evidence
Assessment dossier.

## Unit of analysis

The unit of analysis is a claim connected to a tourism or coastal-service
decision.

The pilot does not evaluate individuals and does not produce a public
ranking of organisations.

## Initial scope

The controlled test will use:

- one tourism or coastal-service context;
- no more than 10 claims;
- no more than 20 evidence objects;
- only lawfully accessible public information;
- the existing Social, Environmental and Territorial QEN axes;
- no QEN weights or automated final score.

The specific organisation, municipality or procedure remains
**TO BE APPROVED** before evidence collection begins.

## Research questions

1. Can each claim be separated from its source and supporting evidence?
2. Can source origin, date and independence be reconstructed?
3. Can the transition from DECLARED to VERIFIED be reproduced?
4. Can missing or contradictory evidence be recorded without assumption?
5. Can a human reviewer understand why evidence was accepted or excluded?
6. Can decision relevance be recorded without automating the decision?

## Evidence workflow

For every claim:

1. assign a unique claim identifier;
2. preserve the exact claim wording;
3. identify claimant, date and decision relevance;
4. classify the source according to `source-policy.md`;
5. collect evidence in `evidence-register.csv`;
6. assign an assessment state;
7. record limitations and contradictions;
8. require human review before any conclusion.

## Permitted assessment states

- DECLARED;
- OBSERVABLE;
- DOCUMENTED;
- VERIFIED;
- PARTIALLY VERIFIED;
- CONTRADICTED;
- NOT VERIFIABLE.

No intermediate state may be presented as regulatory compliance or
operational quality.

## Source requirements

Priority order:

1. primary institutional sources;
2. original organisational documents;
3. independent research or reporting;
4. promotional or unverified material for discovery only.

Dependent reproductions do not count as independent corroboration.

## Acceptance criteria

The pilot is methodologically successful only if:

- 100% of claims have an identified claimant and retrieval date;
- 100% of evidence objects have a recorded source origin;
- every verification state includes a stated method;
- every limitation and contradiction remains visible;
- no claim is upgraded solely through repetition;
- no legal conclusion is inferred from journalistic reporting;
- a human reviewer signs the decision record;
- the audit trail can be reproduced from the registers.

These are completeness controls, not QEN weights or performance scores.

## Stop conditions

Evidence collection must stop if:

- the selected case lacks a sufficiently identifiable primary source;
- verification would require unauthorised access;
- personal or commercially sensitive data would be exposed;
- source rights or provenance cannot be established;
- the case would create an unmanaged conflict of interest;
- the authorised pilot scope would be exceeded.

## Expected outputs

- populated copies of the claims and evidence registers;
- a source and contradiction log;
- a human decision record;
- a methodological validation note;
- a documented list of unresolved limitations.

## Publication and outreach control

The pilot remains internal.

No organisation name, comparison, ranking, outreach message or public
result may be released without explicit approval and a separate review of
sources, rights, proportionality and context.
