## Purpose

This document compares the verified procedural architecture of the Albenga
PUD 19 and PUD 34 procedures with the evidence already acquired for Rimini
and Riccione.

It does not assign a score, produce a legal opinion, modify QEN weights or
evaluate any economic operator.

## Verified facts

### PUD 19

The acquired documentation concerns PUD 19, whose existing activity is
described as a children’s carousel.

The criteria table includes:

- environmental sustainability;
- technical and professional experience related to the concession activity,
  tourism or public establishments;
- a maximum of 5 points for the extracted experience criterion.

The tender rules require:

- a technical proposal responding to the published criteria;
- consideration of safety, hygiene and accessibility;
- a proposed concession duration;
- a financial and economic plan supporting the investments and requested
  duration;
- municipal review of the plan’s sustainability and congruity.

### PUD 34

The acquired documentation concerns PUD 34, associated with the existing
sign “Rada del Gabbiano”.

The criteria table includes:

- environmental sustainability;
- removal of architectural barriers and improved access to the sea and
  bathing for people with disabilities;
- a maximum of 5 points for the extracted accessibility criterion;
- sector-specific technical and professional experience, with an extracted
  maximum of 4 points.

The tender rules require appropriate experience in the relevant
socio-health sector and use the same relationship between proposed
investment, financial plan and requested duration.

## Common procedural architecture

Both Albenga procedures use a chain that can be represented as:

> project criteria → proposed investment → financial and economic plan →
> requested duration → municipal congruity review

The administration may review the proportionality between investments and
duration and may remodulate a requested duration when the underlying
investment assumptions appear overstated.

This is a material governance feature because duration is not treated as an
isolated declaration: it must be supported by documented economic
assumptions and remains subject to administrative review.

## Comparison with Rimini

Rimini currently provides the most complete verified scoring architecture
in the research dossier:

- 100 total points;
- 90 technical points;
- 10 economic points;
- exclusion below 45 technical points;
- 26 discretionary and 64 tabular technical points.

Albenga confirms the use of weighted comparative criteria and commissioner
evaluation. The criterion-by-criterion point structure has now been
reconstructed in `case-03-albenga-criteria-register.csv`.

Albenga therefore cannot yet be placed above or below Rimini in a numerical
ranking.

## Comparison with Riccione

Riccione currently provides verified procedural clarifications rather than
a complete reconstructed scoring matrix.

The verified Riccione evidence covers:

- the minimum economic contribution and absence of a stated maximum
  increase;
- treatment of management experience after corporate reorganisation;
- bidder-specific income;
- evidence attachments outside the technical-report page limit;
- exclusion of avvalimento for the procedure.

Albenga adds a different evidentiary dimension: the express relationship
between project, investment, financial sustainability and concession
duration.

The cases are complementary and not yet numerically comparable.
