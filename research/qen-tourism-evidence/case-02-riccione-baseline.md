## Verified material findings

### EV-RIC-001 — Economic contribution

**Clarification:** PI487273-26, 8 October 2026.

Offers must be equal to or higher than the minimum contribution.
Lower offers are excluded. No maximum increase was specified.

**Interpretation:** material effect on financial-offer strategy.

**Limit:** no inference about final influence without actual offers and
application of the scoring formula.

### EV-RIC-002 — Continuity of experience

**Clarification:** PI484093-26, 8 October 2026.

The operator resulting from transformation, merger or demerger declares
the management years accrued by the continuing activity.

**Interpretation:** corporate reorganisation does not automatically erase
the management history attributed to the resulting operator.

**Limit:** continuity and the resulting points remain subject to proof
and commission assessment.

### EV-RIC-003 — Income criterion

**Clarification:** PI476059-26, 6 October 2026.

Only the applicant's income is considered. Income belonging to other
members of the family unit is excluded.

**Interpretation:** the criterion is individualised around the bidder.

**Limit:** no participant's tax documentation has been reviewed.

### EV-RIC-004 — Evidence attachments

**Clarification:** PI476055-26, 6 October 2026.

Documents supporting statements made for premial criteria are excluded
from the 20-page limit applicable to the technical report.

**Interpretation:** documentary proof does not reduce the narrative space
available for the technical proposal.

**Limit:** exclusion from the page limit does not establish evidential
sufficiency or authenticity.

### EV-RIC-005 — Avvalimento

**Clarification:** PI476047-26, 6 October 2026.

Avvalimento is not applicable to this procedure.

**Interpretation:** bidders cannot use that institution to rely on
third-party capacity.

**Limit:** the conclusion is specific to this tender and cannot be
generalised automatically.
