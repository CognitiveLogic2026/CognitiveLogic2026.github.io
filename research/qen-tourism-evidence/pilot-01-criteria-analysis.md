## Verified scoring architecture

The award uses 100 available points:

| Component | Maximum points |
|---|---:|
| Technical offer | 90 |
| Economic offer | 10 |
| Total | 100 |

A technical score below 45 points results in exclusion.

Within the 90 technical points:

| Evaluation mode | Points |
|---|---:|
| Discretionary | 26 |
| Tabular | 64 |

The six technical families are:

| Code | Criterion family | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| A | Quality and conditions of services offered to users | 35 |
| B | Quality of assets used for the concession | 7 |
| C | Integrated services supporting local tourism, culture and food | 7 |
| D | Diversification, families and accessibility | 20 |
| E | Social policy, worker safety and environmental protection | 14 |
| F | Technical and professional experience | 7 |

## Evaluation mechanics

For discretionary criteria, each commissioner assigns a coefficient from
0.00 to 1.00. The arithmetic mean of the commissioners' coefficients is
applied to the relevant maximum score.

Tabular criteria receive absolute, predefined points according to whether
the offered element is present or absent.

The total is calculated using the compensatory aggregation method.
Technical scores are rounded to three decimal places.

## Economic component

The economic offer is an annual contribution to ordinary maintenance of
the furnishings and greenery of the Parco del Mare.

The maximum annual offer depends on the concession-area band:

| Area band | Maximum annual contribution |
|---|---:|
| Up to 3,000 m² | €1,500 |
| More than 3,000 and under 6,000 m² | €3,000 |
| From 6,000 and under 8,000 m² | €4,500 |
| 8,000 m² or more | €7,000 |

The coefficient is calculated as the bidder's annual offer divided by the
maximum amount for the relevant band.

## Technical-offer evidence architecture

Allegato H separates:

- descriptive material for discretionary criteria;
- commitments or declarations for tabular criteria.

The model expressly requires a copy of the certification or social
responsibility mark claimed under criterion E3.

Several other tabular criteria are expressed as bidder commitments or
declarations. This observation does not prove that subsequent documentary
or performance controls are absent; those controls must be assessed from
the tender minutes, verification records and concession execution.

The technical report may contain no more than 12 pages. Cover, index and
technical or descriptive attachments—such as photographs, renderings and
certifications—are excluded from that page count.

## Financial-plan control

The simplified financial plan must:

- concern only the concession area;
- exclude adjacent private activities;
- include all investments offered;
- include service and management costs;
- include expected revenues;
- demonstrate the coherence and sustainability of the technical offer;
- be formally certified by an authorised professional or institution.

This creates a documented consistency control between the technical offer
and its financial assumptions. It does not by itself verify subsequent
performance.

## QEN interpretation

The documents provide operational evidence for examining whether tender
criteria are:

- defined in advance;
- objectively measurable;
- supported by documentary requirements;
- verifiable during award or execution;
- connected to social, environmental and territorial outcomes.

No QEN score, weight or threshold is assigned or modified in this pilot.

## Limits

The present analysis does not include:

- bidders' submitted technical offers;
- individual commissioners' scores;
- tender-session minutes;
- verification of declarations;
- per-lot rankings;
- final awards;
- concession-execution evidence.

Those records are necessary before assessing how the published criteria
were applied in practice.
