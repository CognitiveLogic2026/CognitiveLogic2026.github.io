# Controlled Pilot Template

## 1. Identification

- Entity:
- Tourism segment:
- Territory:
- Assessment date:
- Reviewer:
- Authorised scope:

## 2. Decision question

What concrete decision should the evidence support?

## 3. Claims

For every claim record:

- exact claim;
- claimant;
- date;
- QEN axis;
- expected evidence;
- potential decision relevance.

## 4. Evidence collection

For every evidence object record:

- origin;
- source class;
- observation or document date;
- retrieval date;
- verification method;
- independence;
- limitations;
- contradiction status.

## 5. Assessment state

Use only:

- DECLARED;
- OBSERVABLE;
- DOCUMENTED;
- VERIFIED;
- PARTIALLY VERIFIED;
- CONTRADICTED;
- NOT VERIFIABLE.

## 6. Human decision record

- decision owner;
- evidence used;
- evidence excluded;
- uncertainty;
- proportionality check;
- final decision;
- review date.

## 7. Publication control

No result, ranking, outreach or public comparison may be released without
explicit approval and a separate review of sources, rights and context.
