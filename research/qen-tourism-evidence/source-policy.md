# Source Policy

## Source classes

### A — Primary institutional source

Legislation, official acts, court decisions, public registers, municipal
documents and publications issued by the competent authority.

Use: authoritative for the content actually stated in the source.

### B — Primary organisational source

Policies, reports, certificates, datasets and statements issued by the
organisation being assessed.

Use: evidence of what the organisation declares or documents, not
independent proof of implementation.

### C — Independent secondary source

Academic research, professional analysis and reporting independent from
the assessed organisation.

Use: corroboration, context or contradiction after checking provenance.

### D — Promotional or unverified source

Marketing material, social content, testimonials, unsourced summaries and
third-party reproductions.

Use: discovery or claim identification only.

## Verification rules

- Record the original source whenever available.
- Preserve title, URL, issuer and relevant dates.
- Distinguish publication date from event or document date.
- Record access limitations and unavailable primary documents.
- Do not upgrade a claim because it appears in multiple dependent sources.
- Record contradictions without resolving them through assumption.
- Assign VERIFIED only when the verification method is explicit and
  reproducible.
- Record uncertainty even when a source is authoritative.
