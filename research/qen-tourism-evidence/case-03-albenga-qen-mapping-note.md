## Purpose

This note maps the 49 verified Albenga criteria to the existing QEN
dimensions:

- Social;
- Environmental;
- Territorial.

The mapping is an analytical classification created by Cognitive Logic. It
is not part of the Municipality of Albenga’s tender documents and does not
change their official point structure.

## Mapping rules

1. The primary axis represents the most direct subject of the criterion.
2. Secondary axes record material cross-axis effects.
3. A criterion may have only one primary axis.
4. Official tender points remain unchanged and separate.
5. No QEN weight is calculated from the official point distribution.
6. Mapping confidence concerns classification clarity, not evidence of
   implementation.
7. Source numbering anomalies remain visible through the original source
   codes and stable internal register identifiers.

## Interpretive findings

Albenga’s two procedures cover all three QEN dimensions, but with different
operational emphases.

PUD 19 includes strong territorial-development, environmental-care and
social-use components.

PUD 34 adds more explicit provisions concerning accessible bathing,
socio-health activities, water efficiency, extended services and
facilities on free beaches.

These are observations about procedural design. They do not demonstrate
that the commitments will be delivered or that one procedure performs
better than another.
