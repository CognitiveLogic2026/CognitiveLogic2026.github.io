# Pilot 01 Case Selection
## Municipality of Rimini — 27 Northern Beach Concessions

**Pilot ID:** QEN-TEA-P01
**Case ID:** RIMINI-CDM-2026-27
**Selection date:** 9 October 2026
**Status:** EVIDENCE BASELINE STARTED
**Publication status:** Internal research; not approved for publication.

## Selected procedure

The pilot concerns the 2026 procedure of the Municipality of Rimini for
27 maritime-state concessions covering shading-only areas on the northern
coast.

The areas complete beach establishments whose service structures are
located on private land.

## Selection rationale

The case provides:

- an official notice from the Municipality of Rimini;
- publication of the tender in the Emilia-Romagna Official Bulletin;
- a later municipal update after the application deadline;
- quantitative and procedural claims suitable for evidence tracing;
- technical themes connected to the existing QEN axes.

## Baseline facts

The official sources state that:

- the procedure concerns 27 concessions;
- the areas are shading-only zones on the northern Rimini coast;
- the concession duration is five years;
- the award method is the most economically advantageous offer;
- 38 applications were received by the deadline;
- technical themes include service quality, accessibility, family
  services, worker safety and environmental protection.

## Interpretation

The case is suitable for testing:

- claim-to-source traceability;
- distinction between tender publication and later procedural phases;
- mapping of technical criteria to QEN evidence axes;
- recording of quantitative facts without unsupported inference;
- separation of official facts from evaluation of procedure quality.

## Limits

The baseline does not establish:

- that 38 applications correspond to 38 different operators;
- how applications were distributed across the 27 lots;
- that every lot received competing applications;
- the identity or characteristics of applicants;
- the final scores, awards or concession holders;
- the legality, fairness or effectiveness of the procedure;
- the complete wording of every criterion in the tender documents.

The two institutional publication channels do not automatically constitute
independent factual corroboration because they may reproduce the same
underlying administrative act.
