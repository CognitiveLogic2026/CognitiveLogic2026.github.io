QEN Framework · Quantificazione Etica Naturale

What is QEN?

QEN is the governance framework developed by Cognitive Logic to organise sources, evidence, scoring and decision traceability in contexts where AI-supported outcomes must remain understandable, reviewable and subject to human authority.

In Italian, QEN stands for Quantificazione Etica Naturale. The original matrix measures social, environmental and territorial value. Its enterprise evolution applies the same evidence-based discipline to AI Governance and Knowledge Governance without turning a contextual score into a moral judgement about people.

Governance before automation.

Purpose

A framework for governable decisions

QEN connects documented sources, explicit criteria, attributable evidence and human responsibility. Its purpose is not to make an AI system appear certain. Its purpose is to make the path from information to decision reconstructable.

Governed sources

Inputs have provenance, scope and authority.

Contextual assessment

Criteria are declared and remain reviewable.

Verifiable evidence

Outputs retain links to supporting records.

Decision traceability

The reasoning path can be reconstructed.

Human authority

Accountable people retain the final decision.

Provider independence

Governance is not tied to one model, vendor or cloud.

Method

How QEN works

QEN structures the path from authoritative input to a decision that remains reviewable, challengeable and accountable.

01 · SourceIdentify authoritative and contextual inputs.
02 · StructureExtract and organise relevant attributes.
03 · AssessmentApply declared criteria and, where appropriate, contextual scoring.
04 · EvidencePreserve supporting records and relationships.
05 · DecisionPresent a result that can be reviewed and challenged.
06 · AuthorityKeep approval, accountability and final judgement human.

Relationship

QEN Framework and QEN Sovereign

They are related, but they are not interchangeable names.

QEN Framework

QEN Framework is the methodological foundation. It defines the method used to organise criteria, evidence, contextual assessment and decision traceability.

QEN Sovereign

QEN Sovereign is the governance infrastructure that operationalises governed knowledge, evidence, traceability, risk classification and human authority in enterprise and institutional contexts.

QEN Framework defines the method. QEN Sovereign provides the governed environment in which the method can be documented, applied, validated and audited.

Limits

What QEN does not claim

  • A QEN score is contextual, not an absolute truth.
  • QEN does not assign moral worth to individuals.
  • QEN does not replace legal, compliance, professional or executive judgement.
  • QEN does not remove the need to verify sources, assumptions and limits.
  • QEN does not make governance dependent on a single AI provider.

Evidence

Evidence before claims

QEN follows a documentation-first and evidence-first approach. Architecture, governance decisions, validation activities and operational records are published as distinct artefacts so that claims can be examined rather than merely asserted.
