Digital Presence Assessment

Before deciding what to rebuild, we measure what can be improved.

A website should not be replaced simply because it is old. It should first be assessed to determine whether its structure, content, technology and visibility are limiting real business opportunities.

01 · Visibility

SEO & Search Visibility

Indexation, SEO structure, page architecture, content, keywords, authority and organic visibility.

02 · Infrastructure

Technical Performance

Mobile usability, speed, Core Web Vitals, technical structure, accessibility and user experience quality.

03 · Representation

Business Representation

We assess whether the website accurately represents products, capabilities, markets, technology, competitive advantages and the organization's real strengths.

04 · Conversion

Commercial Effectiveness

Navigation paths, calls to action, lead generation, service pages and the website's ability to convert interest into qualified business opportunities.

05 · AI Search

AI Search Readiness

Semantic structure, entities, verifiable content, structured data and readiness for emerging AI-based search and answer systems.

06 · Opportunity

Digital Opportunity Score

Evidence is converted into priorities, opportunities, recommended interventions and a roadmap, avoiding unnecessary investment.

How the Assessment works

We begin with external observation. Access is requested only when necessary.

The Assessment separates analysis of the public digital presence from authorized access to client systems and from any subsequent implementation work.

01 · External analysis

Public digital presence

We begin without requesting credentials. We assess structure, indexation, information architecture, content, SEO, performance, authority, conversion and AI Search Readiness through publicly accessible resources.

02 · Evidence

Digital Opportunity Score

We organize findings into verifiable evidence and translate weaknesses, underrepresented value and opportunities into priorities, a diagnostic score and an initial intervention map.

03 · Authorized access

Client data and systems

When it adds diagnostic value, we request named and preferably read-only access to tools such as Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Bing Webmaster Tools or the CMS. Every access requires client authorization.

04 · Decision

Plan and optional implementation

The Assessment produces priorities and an intervention plan. Website changes begin only under an authorized implementation engagement, with controlled access, backups, an intervention log, rollback capability and measurement of results.

Engagement

The entry point is an Assessment, not a website rebuild.

We identify problems, opportunities and priorities first. Only then do we decide whether to optimize, evolve, integrate or rebuild.
