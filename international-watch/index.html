Reuters · International Telecommunication Union

The United Nations opens a global discussion on trust in AI agents

The International Telecommunication Union, the United Nations agency for digital technologies, has launched an international initiative focused on making AI agents identifiable, trustworthy and subject to meaningful human oversight.

The signal is significant: the international debate is moving beyond generative capability toward the governance of systems that can act, decide and interact autonomously.

TechRadar · DigiCert

The issue is no longer adoption. It is governance.

Organizations are deploying AI faster than they can inventory, explain and govern it. Security, identity, provenance and accountability are converging into a single governance challenge.
